About

Trillion AI Tech is an independent technology studio based in Aotearoa New Zealand.

We build applications, games, AI agent systems, tools, and software with a focus on innovation, usability, and modern design principles.

Our mission is to create technology that empowers users and enhances their digital experiences. We believe in transparent development, ethical AI practices, and building products that genuinely solve problems.
