Approach

A workshop, not a funnel

The studio runs on short build cycles: a question becomes a prototype, the prototype earns a name, and only work that survives real use gets released. Progress is written down as it happens.

Build

Own

Independent, self-funded, no platform lock-in on the things we make or the data behind them.

Evolve

Ship, watch, revise. Products are treated as living systems rather than launches.

Signal Console

Move the carrier onto each lit node before its charge fades. Arrow keys, WASD, the on-screen pad, or tap the grid to step.

Console idle.

How to play

  1. Press Start round. You have 45 seconds.
  2. A node lights up somewhere on the grid — move your carrier onto it.
  3. Reaching it fast scores more and builds a streak; letting it fade breaks the streak.
  4. Nodes appear closer together as your streak grows.
↑↓←→WASD Space = pauseR = reset

Touch

  1. Tap anywhere on the grid to step toward that point.
  2. Or use the directional pad below the board.

Scores are held in memory for this visit only — nothing is stored on your device or sent anywhere. If you use reduced-motion settings, the board renders without pulsing and the pace is gentler.

04 / Studio

Independent, from Aotearoa New Zealand

About the studio

Trillion AI Tech is a small independent technology studio working from Aotearoa New Zealand across artificial intelligence, applications, games and interactive systems.

The studio is deliberately compact. Work moves from question to prototype quickly, and the interesting part happens in the middle — the version that runs badly, gets rebuilt, and finally earns a place in front of people. That process is the product line: apps and games as first-party releases, agents and tooling as the infrastructure underneath them.

Nothing has been released yet. Rather than hide that, this site is written as a live record of what is being built, so anyone considering a collaboration can judge the work and not the marketing.

Being based at the far end of the world is part of the operating model: build for the web first, stay lightweight, and design so that distance and bandwidth never determine who gets to use something.

  • One clear mechanic Every product — game or tool — is built around a single idea you can explain in a sentence.
  • Prototype before promise Nothing is announced as finished until it has survived real use in real conditions.
  • Legible machines AI features show their working. If a system acts on your behalf, you can see and stop it.
  • Own the foundation Self-funded and self-hosted where it counts, so the roadmap answers to the work.

Build
